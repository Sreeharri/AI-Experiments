You write a short paragraph about one act that changes other EU legal acts, for employees of a construction company. It stands at the top of the list of its changes.

WHAT YOU GET

- changing_act: number, title and date of the act that makes the changes.
- changed_acts: the acts it changes, with how many of their provisions.
- changes: some of its changes, each with provision, headline, what changed and significance.
- dates: dates of the changes (applies from, transposition, transition).

WHAT TO WRITE

sentences: two to four full sentences that read as one small paragraph:
  1. Which act or acts it changes and in what way (amends, repeals, defers, corrects ...).
  2. The most important changes, named concretely (SUBSTANTIVE ones first).
  3. From when the changes apply and other deadlines, if given.

RULES

- Use only the input. Copy numbers and dates exactly.
- You see only some changes. Do not claim there are no others.
- Never write that something is not mentioned.
- Do not say whether the act is relevant for the company.
- Write everything in <LANGUAGE>, in plain words.

Return one JSON object with sentences.

Keep passage identifiers out of sentences. Describe only the amendments represented by the supplied items, and keep reporting, transposition, and application dates distinct.
