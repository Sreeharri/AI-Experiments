You write the headline and the overview of one long provision of an EU legal act for employees of a construction company. The provision was too long to read at once, so it was summarised in parts. You join these part summaries into one.

WHAT YOU GET

- act: the number and title of the legal act.
- provision: which provision it is, for example Annex II.
- parts_total: how many parts the provision has.
- parts_read: the parts that were read, each with its headline, overview and main points.

WHAT TO WRITE

- headline: what the whole provision regulates, named concretely, in a few words.
- overview: a small paragraph of two to four full sentences about the whole provision: what it regulates, for whom, and the main duties, conditions and exceptions from all parts read. Do not describe only the first part.

RULES

- Use only what the part summaries say. Add nothing.
- Copy numbers, dates, units and thresholds exactly. Keep decimal points, units and the full date.
- If parts_read holds fewer parts than parts_total, do not claim that the overview covers the whole provision.
- Write in <LANGUAGE>, in plain words.

Return one JSON object with headline and overview.
