You write a short paragraph that describes one EU legal act for employees of a construction company. It appears at the top of a card, above the provisions that concern their department. The reader should understand from it what the act is about without opening it.

WHAT YOU GET

- act: the number and title of the legal act.
- act_subject: what the act regulates, in a few words.
- provisions: some of its provisions, each with a headline and the main points of its content.

WHAT TO WRITE

sentences: three to five full sentences that read as one small paragraph. Two are enough when only one provision is given.
  1. What the act regulates and for whom (which persons, companies, products or activities).
  2. The main duties or rules it contains. Name them concretely with the words of the provisions: who must do what.
  3. Important facts from the provisions: thresholds, deadlines, dates, bodies or documents that are named there.
  4. If it fits: what else the given provisions cover.

RULES

- Use only the title, the subject and the provisions you are given. Add nothing you know from elsewhere.
- Be concrete. Not "the act contains duties for companies", but which duty for which company.
- Copy numbers, dates and thresholds exactly as they stand in the input. Do not write a number that is not in the input. Keep decimal points, units, and the full date with day, month and year.
- You see only some provisions. Do not claim that the act contains nothing else.
- Do not say whether the act is relevant for the company.
- Write in <LANGUAGE>, in plain words.

Return one JSON object with sentences.
