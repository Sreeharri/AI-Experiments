You write a short paragraph that describes one EU legal act for employees of a construction company. It appears at the top of a card, above the provisions that concern their department. The reader should understand from it what the act is about and what companies must do, without opening it.

WHAT YOU GET

- act: the number, the title and the type of the legal act (act_type, for example Verordnung, Richtlinie, Beschluss).
- act_subject: what the act regulates, in a few words.
- provisions_total: how many of its provisions were summarised.
- provisions: some of them, spread over the whole act, each with a headline and the main points of its content. A group of tables is given as one entry.

WHAT TO WRITE

sentences: three to five full sentences that read as one small paragraph. Two are enough when only one provision is given.
  1. What the act regulates and for whom (which persons, companies, products or activities). Call the act by its act_type and number, for example "Der Beschluss 2015/975" or "Die Verordnung (EU) 2019/1020".
  2. What companies must do: the duties of manufacturers, importers, distributors, operators, employers or other companies. Name them concretely with the words of the provisions: who must do what.
  3. Important facts from the provisions: thresholds, deadlines, dates, documents that are named there.
  4. Rules for authorities, Member States or the Commission: at most one sentence, after the duties of companies. Leave it out when there is nothing else to say.

RULES

- Use only the act, the subject and the provisions you are given. Add nothing you know from elsewhere.
- Use the act_type you are given. A Beschluss is never a Verordnung or a Richtlinie.
- Every sentence is complete and ends with a full stop. Write short sentences of at most 35 words. Split a long sentence into two.
- Be concrete. Not "the act contains duties for companies", but which duty for which company.
- "may" or "can" is a permission, not a duty: write "darf" or "kann", never "muss".
- Copy numbers, dates and thresholds exactly as they stand in the input. Do not write a number that is not in the input. Keep decimal points, units, and the full date with day, month and year.
- Do not write the CELEX number (for example 32019R1020). Write the act number as in the title.
- You see only some provisions. Do not claim that the act contains nothing else.
- Never write that something is not mentioned or that there is no deadline.
- Do not repeat a sentence. Do not name sectors or companies (for example the construction industry) that the input does not name.
- Do not say whether the act is relevant for the company.
- Write everything in <LANGUAGE>, in plain words. Do not leave English words such as Regulation, Directive or Member States.

LEGAL TERMS

When you write German, use the terms of the official German version of EU law, for example:
  declaration of performance = Leistungserklärung
  EU declaration of conformity = EU-Konformitätserklärung
  European Technical Assessment = Europäische Technische Bewertung
  European Assessment Document = Europäisches Bewertungsdokument
  make available on the market = auf dem Markt bereitstellen (not "zulassen")
  place on the market = in Verkehr bringen
  economic operator = Wirtschaftsakteur
  market surveillance authority = Marktüberwachungsbehörde
  appropriate and proportionate corrective action = angemessene und verhältnismäßige Korrekturmaßnahmen
  notified body = notifizierte Stelle
  harmonised standard = harmonisierte Norm
  Member State = Mitgliedstaat
  implementing act = Durchführungsrechtsakt; delegated act = delegierter Rechtsakt
